# graphika
Theme: The Geometry of Planning and Scheduling Tools

Task: Investigate the shapes and geometry of everyday items used to track time, plan schedules, or organize daily tasks—such as clocks, planners, calendars, or timers.
STAGE 1: Research and Gathering Data
Brainstorm: Write down keywords and list objects used for scheduling and planning.
Primary Research: Take photos of physical objects, measure them, or survey people about the planning tools they use.
Secondary Research: Search online or in books for drawings, diagrams, and background information.
Record Sources: Keep a clear list of every website, book, or image source you use so you can reference them correctly.
STAGE 2: Exploring Geometric Concepts
Identify Shapes: Look for geometric features in your chosen objects, such as angles, symmetry, circles, polygons, or 3D forms.
Apply Graphics Knowledge: Connect your research to topic areas learned in class, such as orthographic projection, constructions, or CAD.
Select Key Information: Choose the most important geometric details required to explain how the object is constructed.
STAGE 3: Final Presentation
Present Original Work: Produce clear graphics that demonstrate your own individual understanding of the topic.
Choose Your Media: Select the most effective format to display your graphics, such as hand drawings, CAD sheets, display boards, or digital slides.
LIST of possible objects:  

1. Analog & Mechanical Timepieces
Classic Wall Clock: Circular geometry, concentric circles, 30°/60° sector divisions ($360^\circ / 12 = 30^\circ$), tangent lines for clock hands, and rotational symmetry.
Traditional Twin-Bell Alarm Clock: Intersecting spheres and cylinders, tangent curves on bell arches, and triangular support legs.
Hourglass / Sand Timer: Conical geometry (two cones meeting at a single vertex point), hyperboloids, axial symmetry, and 3D solids of revolution.
Wristwatch & Buckle Mechanism: Concentric circles, chamfered rectangular cases, strap pin joints, and planar offset links.
2. Physical Planners & Calendars
Triangular Desk Calendar (Tent Flip Calendar): Prismatic 3D forms, triangular profile geometry, and parallel line grids.
Perpetual Wooden Block Calendar: Cubes featuring rotated numbers, polyhedral forms, and isometric block layouts.
Spiral-Bound Planner or Journal: Helical wire coils (3D helix geometry), rectangular grid matrices, and perforated parallel planes.
Fold-out Wall Planner / Map Chart: Dynamic folding geometry, orthographic grid patterns, and tessellation.
3. Mechanical & Digital Timers
Egg / Kitchen Wind-Up Timer: Hemispherical or paraboloid solids, rotational scale markings, and internal gear teeth geometry.
Digital Chess Clock: Dual rectangular prisms, angled display planes, and push-button pivot points.
Stopwatch / Track Timer: Elliptical or teardrop outer contours, radial button alignments, and arc sectors.
4. Organization & Desk Accessories
Hexagonal / Modular Desk Organizer: Hexagonal prisms, tessellating honeycombs, and angled pen slot inclines.
Rotary Card Index (Rolodex): Cylindrical axis, radial card alignment, and circular rotation paths.
Document Tray Stack: Parallel planes, angled sliding tracks, and cantilevered supports.
